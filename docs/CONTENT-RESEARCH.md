# SIF content research register

This register records external sources used to check terminology and sector context. Sources inform editorial framing only; they do not establish SIF credentials, contracts, certifications or delivery capacity.

| Topic | Primary source | Applied to the website |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facility management | [IFMA: What is FM?](https://www.ifma.org/about/what-is-fm/) | IFM is described as coordination rather than a list of unrelated tasks. |
| Integrated FM | [IFMA Knowledge Library](https://knowledgelibrary.ifma.org/integrated-facility-management-vs-facility-management-what-is-the-difference/) | The flagship service explains coordination, responsibility mapping and review. |
| FM management systems | [ISO 41001 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/68021.html) | Used only to understand accepted FM language. No certification is claimed. |
| Healthcare cleaning | [WHO environmental-cleaning guidance](https://www.who.int/publications/b/64662) | Healthcare copy separates ordinary support from specialist clinical work. |
| Airport operations | [ICAO Annex 9](https://store.icao.int/en/annex-9-facilitation) | Airport copy distinguishes terminal support from controlled and regulated activity. |
| Rail station cleaning | [Indian Railways cleaning specification](https://indianrailways.gov.in/railwayboard/uploads/directorate/Environment_Management/SBD_EnHM_080917.pdf) | Rail copy reflects timetable, public-zone and operator controls without claiming eligibility. |
| Warehousing | [OSHA warehousing guidance](https://www.osha.gov/warehousing/hazards-solutions) | Warehouse copy recognises vehicle, equipment and pedestrian movement. |
| Routine facility hygiene | [CDC facility cleaning guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/hygiene/about/when-and-how-to-clean-and-disinfect-a-facility.html) | General copy distinguishes routine cleaning from specialist protocols. |
| Solid-waste rules | [CPCB SWM Rules summary](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/MSW/Salient_features_SWM_Rules.pdf) | Waste references stop at site-defined segregation and agreed handover points. |
| Information architecture benchmark | [BVG India](https://bvgindia.com/) | Reviewed for discovery depth only. No content, claims, design or assets were copied. |

Last editorial review: 23 October 2026.
